Building defensible security programs, not just responding to alerts.

I’m Yaqoob Junejo, a cybersecurity professional based in Pakistan with 7+ years of experience across public sector, telecom and banking. My focus is incident response, cyber threat intelligence, risk assessment and SOC operations — and translating technical findings into data-driven reports and risk advisories for senior leadership.

I currently serve as Assistant Director at a public sector organization, leading IT security strategy and governance. Previously, I was part of the founding team behind Pakistan’s first national sectoral SOC & CERT in the telecom sector, and managed SOC operations at JS Bank.

My work has spanned geopolitical threat monitoring, regulatory compliance (NIST CSF, ISO 27001, CTDISR) and national-scale security gap assessments — with a track record of reducing incident response times and strengthening organization-wide security posture.
